Academics

The school teaches classes IX and X in the High School section, and classes XI and XII in the Higher Secondary section across three streams.

High School — classes IX and X

Sections IX A, IX B, IX C, X A and X B. Subjects taught:

  • Mizo
  • English
  • Mathematics
  • Science
  • Social Science
  • Hindi
  • Scripture
  • Work Education

Higher Secondary — classes XI and XII

Three streams, each with its own subject combination.

Science

Sections A and B in each of classes XI and XII.

Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics and Computer Science.

Arts

Sections A and B in each of classes XI and XII.

History, Education, Political Science, Economics, Sociology and Geography.

Commerce

One section in each of classes XI and XII.

Accountancy, Business Studies, Business Mathematics and Economics.

Please check this against the current prospectus. The subject lists above were taken from the school's own results system, so they reflect what is examined — but the office should confirm the combinations, and add anything taught that is not examined separately.

About the school

Baptist Higher Secondary School has been part of education in southern Mizoram since 1961, teaching students at both secondary and higher secondary level.

Our history

The school was founded in 1961 and has had a long-standing presence in education in the southern part of Mizoram ever since.

What we teach

The school provides education at secondary (high school) and higher secondary level — classes IX and X, and classes XI and XII across the Arts, Science and Commerce streams.

Which classes take admissions

Admission is normally to class IX in the High School section, and to class XI in the Arts, Science and Commerce streams of the Higher Secondary section.

Applying online

Applications are made at the school office. An online application form is planned; when it is built it will live on the school portal, where sign-in and the privacy policy already exist, rather than on this page — because it would collect the personal details of children.
